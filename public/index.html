How it works

Four Meta models work together on every lesson, in about a minute and a half.

  1. 1

    Write

    Muse Spark writes the lesson: scenes, narration, a picture plan, and the parts to point at.

    Muse Spark
  2. 2

    Paint and check

    Muse Image paints every scene at once. Muse Spark looks at each picture, marks each part, and asks for a repaint if it isn't clear.

    Muse Image Muse Spark
  3. 3

    Trace

    SAM 3.1 outlines each part exactly, so the board can trace it in chalk the moment the tutor mentions it.

    SAM 3.1
  4. 4

    Ask

    Raise your hand and ask out loud. Muse Voice Transcribe hears you, and Muse Spark answers by pointing at the picture.

    Muse Voice Transcribe

Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. Narration by Kokoro, an open-weight voice running on your computer.
