How to use Chalk Talk

  1. Pick a topicType anything you're curious about, or play the sample lesson first.
  2. Watch it drawEach part of the picture is traced, coloured in and labelled the moment the tutor mentions it.
  3. Raise your handAsk out loud or type a question at any time. The tutor answers by pointing at the picture.
  4. Find itAt the end, tap the parts on the picture, then answer one quick question.

Made by four Meta models working together: Muse Spark writes the lesson and looks at every picture, Muse Image paints it, SAM 3.1 traces each part, and Muse Voice Transcribe hears your questions.
